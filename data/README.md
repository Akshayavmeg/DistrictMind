# data

Source definitions and **labeled development data** only.

- No district boundary, population, healthcare, rainfall, road, or other dataset is stored here.
- Boundary licensing is unresolved (D-9). No geometry may be copied in until a licensing decision is recorded.
- Any development placeholder must be labeled `DEVELOPMENT PLACEHOLDER` or `SYNTHETIC DEVELOPMENT DATA — NOT REAL DISTRICTMIND EVIDENCE`.
- `data/chroma/` (vector store) is generated locally and git-ignored.
